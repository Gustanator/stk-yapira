Yapira Endurance League 1st Edition

WHAT WAS IT?

This event was planned to be a endurance championship (and was the beta version of STK Enduro League) with twelve previously selected tracks and two races in each round (each round will be one weekend on Saturdays), and a points ranking that is filled with your points that you get each race (the points system is further down the page), and ANYONE COULD PARTICIPATE ON THIS EVENT (without the need of subscription).

SOME PLANNED RULES

1 - DURATION: The races will last normally 20 laps (can change depending the lap time of each track), and the points will be distributed in each race.

2 - AVOID HITTING PLAYERS WHO AREN'T ON THE SAME LAP AS YOU, DAMMIT (if not, you can be PUNISHED with lose of points).

3 - SCHEDULE: All races will start on the Saturdays wrote in the calendar, at 16:30 UTC (5 mins. of tolerance before the race start) and the second race will start 10 mins. after the finish of the first race.

THE PONTUATION SYSTEM

This is the points distributed in the races:

Position Points
1st 25 pts.
2nd 18 pts.
3rd 15 pts.
4th 12 pts.
5th 10 pts.
6th 8 pts.
7th 6 pts.
8th 4 pts.
9th 2 pts.
10th 1 pt.

In disconnection cases, you can receive A PART of the points based on this equation:

[(Amount of laps made by a player) * (Pontuation that the player could win when he disconnects)] / (Amount of laps of the race)

EXAMPLE: In a 25 laps race, if you make 17 laps and be on 6th position when you disconnect, you will receive (18 * 6) / 25 = 108 / 25 = more or less 4 points
